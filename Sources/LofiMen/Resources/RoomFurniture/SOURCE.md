The selected OBJ meshes, matching MTL files, and `furniturebits_texture.png` are
unmodified upstream files. Lofitime imports them with Apple's Model I/O, scales
and positions instances for the room, and recolors the fabric palette at runtime
to match the selected app theme. Assets are bundled; no runtime download is needed.
