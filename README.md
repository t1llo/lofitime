# Lofitime

A lo-fi Pomodoro timer that lives in your menu bar.

Listen to lo-fi music while you work. Finish focus sessions to add plants, books, and furniture to your tiny room.

[Download for Mac](https://github.com/t1llo/lofitime/releases/latest) · [Website](https://lofi.beffa.xyz) · [How to use it](https://lofi.beffa.xyz/docs/)

## Features

- Set your own focus and break lengths with a menu-bar Pomodoro timer.
- Listen to lo-fi radio with or without a running timer.
- Build out your tiny room with plants, books, and furniture as you finish sessions.
- Look back at your saved sessions and optionally sync history across Macs.
